## Limitations and discrepancies

- Folder names were not treated as department assignments. Unlisted Municipal Document Repository folders stay in `needs-department-review` with a blank department slug.
- Equal filename and size is recorded in `duplicateCandidate` and does not merge rows. The only source statement of identical contents is the comment in `completedWorks.ts` about the two Zone 6 files dated 17 June 2017. Those remain separate physical files.
- `driveFileId` and `driveUrl` are empty for every row. Existing Google Drive URLs are quoted in `notes` so the current links are not lost.
- Generated catalogue records are separate rows. They were not matched to local audit reports, bye-laws, budgets, or minutes by similar titles.
- Government Orders row GR/2025/044 has no file. The Public Documents filters for resolutions, RTI, and tenders still have no file list. Budget 2024-25 is a featured section with no file. None of those empty sections became a file row.
- `completedWorks.ts` says "List of Completed Work Zone 6 13.06.2017" is not in the Completed Works folder. That name is not a current website listing, so it has no CSV row.
- Site search is not a separate location. Dastavez and NCAP notes record that those pages are searchable.
- The earlier audit counted 2,267 files. This draft counts 2,275. The additional 8 files are under `documents/ncap/documents/`. They are separate from the published library at `ncap/documents/` and were not in that audit folder listing.
- The comparison used exact relative paths. Filename casing of listed files matched the disk in the earlier audit; this draft stores the on-disk path.
- No PDF contents were opened. Google Drive folder contents were not inventoried.
- Needs Review and Duplicate Review stay unpublished, matching `standingCommitteeMinutes.ts`.
